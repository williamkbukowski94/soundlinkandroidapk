A few things to know.

Is the app on Google Play?

Not yet. This is a directly downloadable, debug-signed Android test build.

Can I use Spotify or another music app?

This version plays local, unprotected music files selected inside soundLInk. Capturing audio from other apps is not included.

Do I need internet while listening?

No. The phones communicate over their local Wi-Fi network. You need internet to download the APK.

What should I expect from synchronization?

The app uses a shared timeline, a three-second buffer, drift correction, and manual timing adjustments. Actual acoustic alignment depends on your phones and speakers and has not been verified. Start with two devices and calibrate before adding more. This test build supports up to eight phones, including the host.
